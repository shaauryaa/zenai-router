> Conveyance Reimbursements:

e Airport Transfers: Local transport to and from airport / railway
station, both at place of posting and the location visited, would

e Own Car: Rate of reimbursement for fuel and other expenses for

exceeding total cost of travel as per Travel Entitlement. In case,
more than one employee is traveling together in the same

Cost Efficiency:
Employees are encouraged to:
e Avail Low-cost carriers for business travel

e Consider Discounted / Advance Coupons in case of frequent
travel

e Use local taxis and cabs/radio cabs

e Avoid hiring full day cars where the usage is specific to Hotel -
Office - Hotel

Cancellations:

e In case of change in travel plan, it is the responsibility of the
employee to inform the Quest 2 travel team as well as the guest
house team so that the reservation is cancelled.

General Guidelines: Employee Travel:

It is important to be aware that all employees must fly on the most
optimum fare for all business-related travel. Employees are encouraged
to book tickets at least 7 days prior to their travel.

Employees Flying Together
No more than three (3) Directors & above should travel on the same

flight. This is important from a business continuity and risk management
perspective.

Travel Approval and Booking Process

Employees should book tickets for business travel through Quest2Travel
on Timescape.

Travel Advance

If required, an Employee may request for a travel advance to his
Functional Director. The same may be approved by the Functional
Director based on expenses likely to be incurred on a case to case basis.
The Travel Advance will be adjusted from employee’s next monthly
salary. The actual expense incurred on travel by the Employee would be
reimbursed on submission of bills as per the entitlement.

Settlement of Travel Expenses:

Employees are encouraged to settle travel expenses immediately on
completion of travel. Any delay in settling travel expenses beyond 45
days will be recovered from salary.

Others:

Where business travel is necessitated for Vendors the related expenses
for such travel must be submitted either via Timescape or as part of the
vendor’s billing procedures, as applicable.
