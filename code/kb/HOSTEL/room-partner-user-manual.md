## Page 1

Room Partner User Manual - Student login Step 1: Login to the MyCamu student portal
Student have to open the MyCamu student app or the CAMU web URL https://student.bennetterp.camu.in/ and log in by clicking on “Login with Institution Email” using your BU email credentials. Use your own student account to log in, not the parent account.
Step 2: Go to the menu Room partner selection option

## Page 2

Step3: Please select the option of room category which is configured for your program
Step 4: If it is triple sharing, you will be able to select 2 of your preference and if it twin sharing you will be able to select 1 of your preference from the available students

## Page 3

Step 5: If you select lesser than or more than the total number of students based on the room category it will throw an error message.
Step 6: Once you select the room partners it will send the request to them to accept or reject the same

## Page 4

Step 7: Once the requested partners select the request based on the category students will be grouped
Students will be able to see their group in the screen once the requested partners have accepted the request Thank you
