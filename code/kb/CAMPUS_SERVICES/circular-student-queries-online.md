Subject: Online Helpdesk for Student Queries

To facilitate quick and efficient resolution of queries related to the Registrar's Office,
students are advised to use the Bennett University Online Helpdesk. The helpdesk is
designed to streamline communication and ensure timely assistance for various requests.

Steps to Raise a Query:
1. Login to the helpdesk at https: //helpdesk.bennett.edu.in/open.php.
2. Opt for Registry Helpdesk under the department section.
3. Select the relevant category and subcategory based on your query.
4. Refer to the attached user manual for guidance on generating a helpdesk ticket.
