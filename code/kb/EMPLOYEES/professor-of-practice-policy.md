Policy for the Appointment of Professor of Practice

Policy Owner: HR Department, BU

Policy Effective: 01% July, 2023
Policy No.: BUHR/

Purpose:
To engage distinguished professionals with extensive industry experience, innovative practices, and
real-world knowledge for the benefit of students and faculty at Bennett University.

Eligibility Criteria:

1. Qualifications:
e Demonstrated expertise in relevant fields such as industry, entrepreneurship, public
service, creative arts, or other professional domains.
e A minimum of 15 years of experience in the relevant field.
e No mandatory academic qualifications such as a Ph.D.

2. Distinction:
e Must have a proven track record of excellence with national or international
recognition in their area of expertise.

3. Exclusions:

e Individuals engaged in teaching positions in other universities or institutions are not
eligible.

Appointment:

1. Selection Committee:

A dedicated committee includes:
e The Vice Chancellor
The Dean of the school
Dean of Academic Affairs
Dean of Research & Consultancy
An external member
Head- HR, will evaluate candidates based on their industry reputation, achievements,
and alignment with university goals.

eeeee

2. Term of Appointment:

e The tenure will be contractual, ranging from 1 to 3 years, extendable based on
performance and university requirements.

3. Honorarium and Benefits:

e The honorarium will be commensurate with the candidate’s expertise and subject to
university policies and norms.

Roles and Responsibilities:

1. Teaching and Mentorship:
e Delivering specialized courses, workshops, and training sessions.
e Mentoring students and faculty in practical applications and innovations.

2. Collaboration:
e Bridging the gap between academia and industry through projects, internships, and
consultancy.

3. Knowledge Sharing:
e Facilitating seminars, guest lectures, and industry-oriented discussions.

Guidelines:

1. Title Usage:
e The title “Professor of Practice” is honorary and cannot be used for claiming regular
faculty positions or academic seniority.

2. Non-Regular Appointment:
e Professors of Practice will not be part of the regular teaching cadre.

3. Workload:
e Flexible workload will be determined by the university’s academic and operational
needs.
Interpretation:

Any point not specifically covered under the above guidelines shall be referred to HR.
The interpretation of this policy rests exclusively with the University and the decision taken by the
University shall be final and binding on all.

Disclaimer:

Management reserves the right to amend or revise this policy at its discretion, and such changes will

Any exceptions to the policy require written approval from the Chancellor.
