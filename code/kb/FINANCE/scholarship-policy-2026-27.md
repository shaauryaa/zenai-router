SCHOLARSHIP POLICY

Academic Year 2026-27

Purpose: Bennett University offers various scholarship schemes to attract a meritorious and diverse category of
students in specific programs as decided by the University.
Introduction:
The award of scholarship will be recommended by the Admission Committee, Fee Committee, or Finance Committee
and duly approved by the Executive Council. Seats and scholarships are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis and
are subject to availability at the time of fee payment. Early application and timely admission decisions improve
admission and scholarship prospects.
In cases where a student qualifies for multiple scholarships, the scholarship with the highest monetary value, as per
university guidelines, will be awarded to ensure fairness and alignment with policy.
The details of the scholarships offered will be included as part of the admission offer extended to candidates upon
selection.
Bennett University offers the following categories of Scholarships:

1. GON-ADMISSION SCHOLARSHIP

Bennett University offers the following categories of Scholarships:
