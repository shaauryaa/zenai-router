BENNETT UNIVERSITY FEE REFUND POLICY
ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-27

PS BENNETT NAAC

Bennett University (“BU”/"University”) Fee Refund Policy for new admission of students
during the AY 2026-27 is based on Fee Refund Policy notified by University Grants
Commission (UGC) vide Notification D.O. No.R2-71/2022(CPP-ID (C-114546) dated June 12,
2024.

This Policy shall be applicable to all Programmes run by the University.
1. Procedure for admission cancellation/withdrawal

1.1 A-student duly admitted to Ist semester of any programme run by the University may
apply for cancellation of his/her admission and/or for withdrawal from studies at any time
during the academic year of admissions.

1.2 Application for cancellation of admission/withdrawal should be made by submitting
the duly filled-in refund application form in the prescribed format (available on the website),
duly signed, in person by the student and parents/guardian. Only written applications will be
accepted.

1.3 The application for cancellation/withdrawal of admission, to be emailed by the student
to concerned departments including the Dean of the respective school, Marketing &
Admissions department, the Finance Department, and the Registrar for approval in
accordance with the policy guidelines.

1.4 Incasea programme is called off before the start of the academic session (1st semester)
due to any reason(s), full fee deposited by the student shall be refunded.

1.5 The refund of fees shall be processed in accordance with the policy issued by the
University and in accordance with the policy notified by the University Grants Commission
(UGC).

be initiated as deemed appropriate.

2. Procedure for Refund

2.1 Refund shall be processed by Finance Department through online mode/ bank transfer
after verifying all necessary documents including duly filled in application form or documents,
necessary approvals and after completion of no dues process.

2.2 No interest shall be payable on refund of any fees.

2.3. In any other case which is not covered in these rules, the decision would be taken by
the Vice Chancellor.

2.4 Any disputes in this regard are subject to the legal jurisdiction of District of Gautam
Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh.

Refund of Sports Charges for Day Scholars:

For the day scholars, the following shall be the procedure for the refund of Sports Charges in
only in case of withdrawal of admission:

1. Till the date of Registration, a candidate is entitled for full refund of Sport charges paid.

2. From the date of Registration for the programme and having paid the Sports charges, the
amount shall be refunded on pro-rata basis. For calculation of pro-rata deduction/refund,
Semester shall be counted of 5 months duration (part of the month starting from day one will
be considered full month for deduction).

3. Once registered, the refund shall be processed on completing the no due clearance issued
by the Sports Officer.
