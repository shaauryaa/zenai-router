# Annexure I

## RULES FOR LATE FEE PAYMENT

### 1. Late Registration Window

Students who fail to clear their dues by the prescribed deadline may be permitted to register during the **Late Registration Window (25 July 2026 to 29 July 2026)** upon payment of a **late registration fee of ₹1,000**.

### 2. Final Date for Late Fee Payment

**29 July 2026** shall be the final date for fee payment under the **Late Registration Window**.

### 3. Fee Payment After 29 July 2026

Fee payment after **29 July 2026** shall be permitted only in exceptional circumstances, subject to:

- Prior approval of the **Registrar**.
- Payment of a **late registration fee of ₹5,000**.

The decision of the **Registrar** shall be final.

### 4. Failure to Complete Fee Payment and Registration

Students who fail to complete fee payment and registration in accordance with the above provisions shall have their **ERP account/profile deactivated** and shall be treated as **not registered for the semester**.

Any academic loss arising from non-registration shall be the **sole responsibility of the student**.
